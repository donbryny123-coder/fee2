Help centre

Answers for the tasks you do most often.

Add a student

Open Classes, choose the class, then use Add student above the student list. Enter the name and select Male or Female. Enrollment updates as soon as the student is saved.

Switch schools

Go to Settings → Schools and choose a school. Its students, fees, and payments are kept separate from other schools.

Recover your records

In Settings, download a backup for each school and store it outside this app folder. If the folder is deleted, restore the app, select the school, then upload its backup under Backups and recovery.

Enrollment counts

The Enrollment page shows total students, boys, girls, and students whose gender has not yet been recorded. Existing students are included in the total.
